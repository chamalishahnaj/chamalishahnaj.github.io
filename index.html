Research

Projects

Green tea leaf extracts as a natural antibacterial against Escherichia coli

My MS thesis tested aqueous extracts of green tea leaves at four concentrations (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) against E. coli isolated from cloacal swab samples, using agar well and disk diffusion and MIC assays on Mueller-Hinton agar. Inhibition rose with dose, from 10.31 ± 1.66 mm to 22.19 ± 1.78 mm. The strongest extract matched ceftriaxone (22 mm), and even the weakest beat gentamicin (6 mm), to which the isolate was resistant. Results were confirmed with one-way ANOVA (p < 0.05).

Buffalo diseases and indigenous cattle performance in Jaintapur, Sylhet

A cross-sectional field survey of cattle- and buffalo-owning households. I recorded productive traits (milk yield, body weight, lactation length) and reproductive traits (calving interval, gestation length, age at puberty), and estimated disease prevalence, morbidity, and mortality in buffaloes. Foot-and-mouth disease, parasitic infestation, and respiratory disease were the main problems, linked to management practices. Crossbred cattle gave more milk with shorter calving intervals, while indigenous cattle adapted better and got sick less often.

DVM research project, Faculty of Veterinary, Animal and Biomedical Sciences, Sylhet Agricultural University (2022).
